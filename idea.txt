THE SUN GATE
A temple escape room starring Indiana Jones


1. PREMISE

Indiana Jones follows an old map to a jungle temple said to hold a golden idol.
The moment he steps inside, the entrance door slams shut behind him. A giant
hourglass in the hall flips over and the sand starts to fall. When it empties,
the temple seals forever.

The only way out is the Sun Gate at the far side of the temple, and it opens
only with the Sun Medallion. Indy has to find the medallion, grab the idol, and
reach daylight before the sand runs out.

The temple was built to confuse thieves. Its passages twist, so leaving a room
west can bring you back in from the north. A low tunnel also runs under the
entrance hall, linking the crypt directly to the bridge.


2. ROOMS (mapped to layout.txt)

R0  Entrance Hall (START)
    The sealed door, the giant hourglass and a stone guardian face.
R1  Cistern Passage
    A dripping corridor where a stone plug dams an underground spring.
R2  Crypt
    Dusty tombs. The Sun Medallion rests on a sarcophagus.
R3  Idol Altar
    The golden idol sits on a pedestal under carvings of snarling monkeys.
R4  Rope Bridge
    A rotting bridge over a chasm. The Sun Gate is at the far end.
R5  Sun Gate (LOCKED, opened by the medallion)
    Daylight and the jungle beyond. Freedom.


3. ITEMS AND NPC

           The gate's carved sun swallows the medallion and the stone grinds open.
whip       (Cistern Passage)     used on the Rope Bridge
           Indy cracks the whip around a beam and swings over the broken planks.
idol       (Idol Altar)          used at the Sun Gate, required to win
           Indy holds the idol up to the daylight. It was worth it.

guardian   (Entrance Hall, NPC)
           The sand falls fast, stranger. Water turns the wheel and the wheel
           lifts the sand. Find the sun to leave, and take what you came for.


4. SIMULATION (Milestone Two)

Resources at the start:  sand 7, water 0

Machines:
    spring      Cistern Passage  PRODUCER   makes 3 water          starts OFF
    waterwheel  Entrance Hall    CONVERTER  2 water -> 2 sand      starts OFF
    hourglass   Entrance Hall    CONSUMER   uses 1 sand            starts ON

The hourglass is the escape room timer. Seven grains is not enough time to grab
the medallion and the idol and reach the gate. The player has to buy time by
opening the spring and starting the waterwheel, which lifts sand back into the
hourglass. With both running, the sand rises by 1 each tick instead of falling.

Balance check:
    Skip the machines and the sand runs out on tick 7, just before the gate.
    Fastest winning route (10 ticks): toggle waterwheel, west, toggle spring,
    west, take idol, east, take medallion, east, use medallion, east.
    Indy walks out with 13 sand left.


5. END CONDITIONS

WIN   Escape: Indy is at the Sun Gate (R5) carrying the idol.
LOSE  Sealed in: the sand reaches 0.
